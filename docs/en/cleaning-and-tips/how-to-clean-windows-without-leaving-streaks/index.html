Tutorial: How to Clean Windows Without Leaving Streaks

Home Decor 20/09/2026 4 min read

Learn Step by Step: How to Clean Windows Without Leaving Streaks

Introduction

Cleaning windows may seem like a simple task, but leaving them streak-free requires the right technique and materials. In this tutorial, you will learn a practical and efficient method to make your windows and glass shine without spending too much time or effort.

The process takes about 30 minutes and is classified as easy, ideal for those who want professional results at home. We will cover everything from choosing the right products to the final details to ensure your windows are spotless.

Necessary Materials

  • Microfiber cloths (2 units)
  • Neutral detergent or specific glass cleaner
  • Warm water
  • Squeegee (optional but recommended)
  • Bucket
  • Cleaning gloves

Step by Step

  1. Prepare the cleaning solution: Mix 1 liter of warm water with 5 ml of neutral detergent in the bucket. If you prefer, learn how to use natural cleaning products in our guide here.
  2. Clean the glass: Wet a microfiber cloth in the solution and wipe the glass, removing superficial dirt. Be careful not to leave the glass too wet.
  3. Use the squeegee: If you have a squeegee, run it over the glass from top to bottom in straight motions. This helps remove excess water and prevents streaks.
  4. Dry with the second cloth: Use the second dry microfiber cloth to remove the remaining water, always using circular or straight motions.
  5. Check for streaks: If streaks appear, wipe again with the dry cloth or use a more diluted detergent solution.
  6. Clean the frames: Don’t forget the frames and tracks. Use a damp cloth and, if necessary, a small brush.
  7. Finish up: Give a final check to ensure all areas are clean and dry.
  8. Dispose of materials: Wash the cloths and store them for the next cleaning. Dispose of the dirty water properly.

Important Tips

Do not clean windows under direct sunlight, as this can cause streaks when drying too quickly.
Use high-quality microfiber cloths to avoid lint and streaks.
For high windows, use safety equipment or seek professional help.

Common Mistakes

  • Using newspaper: Although it’s an old tip, it can leave marks and is not as effective as microfiber cloths.
  • Excess product: Using too much detergent or solution can leave residues on the glass.
  • Ignoring frames: Frames accumulate dust and can ruin the final look.
  • Not drying completely: Leaving wet areas can result in streaks after drying.

Completion Checklist

  • Clean and streak-free glass
  • Sanitized frames and tracks
  • Washed and stored cloths
  • Dirty water properly disposed of
  • Check for streaks under different light angles
  • Equipment stored in a safe place

FAQ

Can I use vinegar to clean windows?

Yes, vinegar is a great natural product for cleaning glass. Mix equal parts water and white vinegar for an effective solution.

How to avoid streaks when cleaning windows?

Use microfiber cloths and avoid excess water or product. Drying the glass thoroughly after cleaning is also essential.

What is the best time to clean windows?

Prefer cloudy days or periods when the sun is not shining directly on the glass.

Can I use specific chemical products?

Yes, but always follow the manufacturer’s instructions and use regulated products, as indicated by Anvisa.

Conclusion

By following these steps, your windows will be clean and streak-free, ensuring a brighter and cozier environment.
