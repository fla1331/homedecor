Robot Vacuums: Are They Worth Buying? - Complete Review

Home Decor 26/08/2026 4 min read

Complete Review: Are Robot Vacuums Worth Buying?

Introduction

Busy routines often push house cleaning to the background. Dirty floors, pet hair, and accumulated dust become chronic issues. That's why I decided to test robot vacuums - do they really make life easier or are they just another expensive gadget?

After three months of daily use with three different models (including famous brands and more affordable options), I can say there are huge differences between them. Some truly revolutionized my cleaning routine, while others left me frustrated.

In this complete review, I'll share my real experience, show comparative technical data, and answer the question everyone asks: are robot vacuums worth the investment? For pet owners, parents, or anyone who hates vacuuming, this analysis might change your perspective on automated cleaning.

About the Product

Robot vacuums are autonomous devices that map environments and perform vacuuming without human intervention. Current models go far beyond the first "blind" robots - they now feature LiDAR technology for precise navigation, smart apps, and even voice recognition.

The compact design (usually between 8-10cm tall) allows them to reach under furniture we wouldn't even think to clean. The battery lasts long enough to clean medium-sized apartments on a single charge, and premium models even return to their base automatically when they need to recharge.

Main Benefits

  • Time savings: While the robot vacuums, you can work, take care of kids, or simply relax
  • Daily automatic cleaning: Set fixed schedules and never accumulate dust again
  • Hard-to-reach areas: Cleans under beds, sofas, and cabinets we normally can't access
  • App control: Adjust settings, create maps, and monitor cleaning remotely
  • Quiet operation: Much less noise than a traditional vacuum
  • Effective against pet hair: Ideal for pet owners
  • Low energy consumption: Uses less power than conventional models

Pros and Cons

Advantages:

  • Consistent daily cleaning with no effort
  • Exceptional for pet hair and dust
  • Programmable and remotely controllable
  • Access to difficult areas (under furniture)
  • Some models mop while vacuuming

Disadvantages:

  • High initial investment for top models
  • Doesn't completely replace manual cleaning
  • Regular maintenance required (brush and filter cleaning)
  • May struggle with thick carpets
  • Some get stuck on wires or small objects

My Experience

When I started testing robot vacuums, I was amazed by the amount of dust and hair they collected daily - even on days I thought my home was "clean." In the first month, the premium model removed about 200g of dust/dirt per week from my 80m² apartment.

The biggest difference was noticing improved air quality, especially for allergy sufferers. With the robot running daily, there was less dust circulating. My cat's hair, which used to accumulate in corners, practically disappeared.

However, I learned that you can't just set it and forget it. The dustbin needs emptying every 2-3 cleanings (in homes with pets), and brushes require weekly cleaning to maintain efficiency. I also had to "prepare" the environment - picking up loose wires and small objects the robot might swallow.

"The biggest mistake users make is thinking robot vacuums are 'plug and play.' Regular maintenance is crucial for longevity" - Home appliance expert from Inmetro

Market Analysis

Comparing main models available in Brazil:

1. Xiaomi Roborock S7 (R$ 3,299): Considered the best cost-benefit, with precise LiDAR navigation, 2500Pa suction, and vibrating mopping. In my experience, it had the best overall performance, though the app could be more intuitive.

2. Roomba i3+ (R$ 3,999): Traditional brand with genius self-emptying system (up to 60 days without changing the bag). However, navigation is less precise than Roborock's and the price is steeper.

3. Positivo Vision (R$ 1,199): More affordable domestic option. While it has fewer features (random navigation, no app), it handles basics well for small apartments. Ideal for trying the technology without major investment.

To compare official specifications, visit the Inmetro website for energy performance and noise data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can robot vacuums replace regular vacuums?

Not completely. While they maintain daily cleaning, it's still recommended to use a traditional vacuum 1-2 times per month for areas robots don't reach well (corners, stairs) or deeper cleanings.

Do they work well with pets?

Yes, especially models with special hair brushes. My cat sheds a lot and the robot reduced my sweeping time by 80%. But you'll need to empty the dustbin more frequently.

How long does the battery last?

Varies from 60 to 180 minutes depending on the model. Premium models recharge automatically and resume where they left off. For large apartments (100m²+), I recommend models with at least 150 minutes of runtime.

Does it require maintenance?

Yes, essential to clean brushes weekly and replace filters every 3-6 months (depending on use). Neglecting maintenance drastically reduces efficiency and the device's lifespan.

Conclusion

After months of testing different models, I conclude that robot vacuums are worth the investment - especially for those who:

  • Have pets
  • Suffer from dust allergies
  • Have limited cleaning time
  • Have smooth floors (or low-pile rugs)

For tight budgets, start with a basic model (like the Positivo Vision). If you can invest more, the Xiaomi Roborock offers the best balance between price and technology. Roomba models with self-emptying are ideal for maximum convenience.

Remember: no robot replaces 100% of human cleaning, but it can eliminate 80% of the tedious work. In my opinion, the time saved and improved quality of life justify the investment.
