Guide: How to Organize the Fridge Efficiently

Home Decor 07/12/2026 4 min read

How to Organize the Fridge Efficiently: Complete Guide

Keeping the fridge organized is much more than an aesthetic issue - it is an essential practice for those who want to optimize space, save time in daily life, and avoid food waste. In Brazil, where temperatures are high for most of the year, good fridge organization becomes even more important to ensure proper food preservation. Additionally, a well-arranged fridge allows you to:

  • Find what you need quickly
  • Better control expiration dates
  • Avoid cross-contamination
  • Keep food fresh for longer
  • Reduce electricity consumption

In this complete guide, we will teach you practical techniques used by organization experts to transform your fridge into a functional and efficient space.

Step-by-Step Guide to Organizing the Fridge

1. Complete Cleaning and Sanitization

Start by completely emptying the fridge. Remove all shelves and drawers and wash them with warm water and neutral soap. To disinfect, use a solution of 1 tablespoon of bleach per liter of water. Dry well before putting them back. This step should be done at least once a month.

2. Smart Division by Temperature Zones

Each area of the fridge maintains a different temperature. Take advantage of this characteristic to store each type of food in the ideal location:

Location Temperature Recommended Foods Extra Tip
Top shelf Coolest (around 4°C) Dairy products (milk, cheeses, yogurts), eggs Keep cheeses in airtight containers
Middle shelf Intermediate (5-6°C) Leftovers, ready-to-eat foods, cold cuts Use transparent containers for easy viewing
Bottom shelf Coolest near the freezer Raw meats, fish, poultry Place in containers to avoid leaks
Drawers Least cold (around 8°C) Fruits, vegetables, and greens Separate fruits from vegetables to avoid ripening
Door Warmest area Condiments, sauces, beverages Avoid placing milk in the door - temperature varies too much

3. Efficient Storage System

Invest in accessories that maximize space:

  • Airtight glass containers: Ideal for storing opened foods and leftovers
  • Fridge organizers: Drawers, extra shelves, and dividers
  • Etageres: To make use of shelf height
  • Door hooks: To hang plastic bags and small items

4. Expiration Control and Rotation

Apply the FIFO method (First In, First Out):

  1. Place newer foods behind older ones
  2. Label with dates using tape or labels
  3. Do a weekly review to discard expired items

5. Avoid Overcrowding

Keep at least 30% of the space free to:

  • Allow proper cold air circulation
  • Facilitate food visibility
  • Prevent the motor from overworking

Golden Extra Tips

  • For fruits and vegetables: Wrap herb stems in damp paper towels and store in containers. Place absorbent paper in drawers to increase durability.
  • For meats: Divide into portions before freezing and label with dates.
  • Ideal temperature: Keep between 4°C and 5°C in the main compartment.
  • Odors: Place half a lemon with cloves or a bowl of baking soda to neutralize smells.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to prevent food from drying out in the fridge?

To keep food moist and fresh for longer:

  • Use airtight containers or wrap in plastic wrap
  • For cold cuts and cheeses, use parchment paper before wrapping
  • Keep fridge humidity regulated (70-80% for vegetables)

Can I store tomatoes in the fridge?

It's best to store tomatoes outside the fridge, in a cool and ventilated place. Refrigeration alters their texture and flavor. If you need to store them in the fridge, place them in the vegetable drawer and consume within 2-3 days.

How many times a year should I defrost the fridge?

For frost-free models, it's not necessary. For traditional models, it's recommended to defrost when the ice layer reaches 0.5cm thick (usually every 2-3 months). In winter, it may be less frequent.

Where to store bread and cakes in the fridge?

Bread tends to dry out in the fridge - it's best to freeze in slices. Cakes with cream or frosting should be kept on the middle shelf, in a closed container, for up to 3 days. Dry cakes are better kept at room temperature.

Conclusion

Organizing the fridge efficiently is a habit that brings numerous benefits: from savings at the supermarket to convenience in daily life. With the techniques presented in this guide, you will not only optimize space but also extend the shelf life of food and keep your family safer regarding proper food preservation. Remember: small weekly adjustments are more effective than occasional major reorganizations.
